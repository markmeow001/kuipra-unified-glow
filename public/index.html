Frequently Asked Questions
What is Kuipra Communications?

Kuipra Communications is a professional brand strategy and digital marketing agency based in Greater Vancouver, BC, specializing in social media management, content creation, brand strategy, personal branding, and local SEO & AI search optimization (GEO) — in both English and Chinese.

What marketing services do you offer in Vancouver?

Four core services: Social Media Management (strategy, content and community across platforms), Content Creation (copy, visuals and video), Brand Strategy & Personal Branding, and Local SEO & AI Search Optimization (GEO) — plus web design and development.

Where are you located and which areas do you serve?

We are based in Greater Vancouver, British Columbia, and serve clients across Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey and the rest of BC — with remote collaboration available across Canada.

Do you run campaigns in both English and Chinese?

Yes — bilingual campaigns are our specialty. We develop strategy and creative natively in English and Chinese, covering Western platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn) and Chinese platforms (Xiaohongshu/RED, WeChat) for the Metro Vancouver market.

Do you provide event marketing services?

Yes. We provide event marketing and amplification in Vancouver: pre-event buzz, influencer outreach and partnerships, live social media coverage, OOH strategy, and post-event content that extends an event's reach for weeks.

Who leads the Kuipra team?

Kuipra Communications is led by Principal Consultant Charlene Ling, an award-nominated media professional, supported by a bilingual team covering strategy, content, design and development.

How do I contact Kuipra Communications?

Email info@kuipra.ca, call 672-964-1888, or use the contact form on this site. We typically reply within 24 hours.

Where can I learn more about your marketing approach?

The Kuipra Journal publishes bilingual articles on brand strategy, Vancouver marketing, campaign teardowns and AI marketing — read it at kuipra.ca/blog.
