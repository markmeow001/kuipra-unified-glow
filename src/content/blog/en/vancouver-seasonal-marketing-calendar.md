Every January we watch the same thing happen: a local business scrambles to post something for Lunar New Year three days before the holiday, goes quiet until a rushed Mother's Day promo, then wonders in December why the "seasonal content" never moved the needle.

The problem isn't effort. It's that seasonal marketing gets treated as a series of surprises instead of what it actually is — a calendar you can see a year out. Here's how we build one for Vancouver.

## Winter: the year starts before the year starts

**Lunar New Year** (late January to mid-February, depending on the year; Saturday, February 6 in 2027) is, in our experience, Metro Vancouver's biggest cultural marketing moment outside of Christmas. Organizers expected around 100,000 spectators at the 2026 Chinatown Spring Festival Parade ([Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/vancouver-chinatown-lunar-new-year-parade-road-closures-2026)), Richmond's malls run major activations, and gift-giving and dining-out spending spikes across the region. If you serve Metro Vancouver, this belongs on your calendar — and done properly, not as a red-and-gold template. We've written a full guide on [marketing to Vancouver's Chinese community](/blog/marketing-to-vancouver-chinese-community/) if this moment matters to you. For retailers, the [T&T teardown](/blog/tt-supermarket-bilingual-retail-teardown/) shows the festival changing operations, not just posters.

**Valentine's Day** works for far more than restaurants and florists — any brand with a gifting or treat-yourself angle can play. Gifting businesses live on these peaks, as the [Purdys teardown](/blog/purdys-chocolatier-teardown/) shows. **Family Day BC** (the third Monday of February) is a quieter, distinctly local moment: family-oriented content and long-weekend offers land well precisely because fewer brands bother.

February 2027 is crowded: Lunar New Year on the 6th, Ramadan expected to begin around the 8th, Valentine's Day and Family Day on the 14th and 15th, and the Lantern Festival on the 20th. Decide early which moment you lead with. If Muslim customers are part of your market, many will be fasting through daylight hours that month, so evening offers fit better than lunch deals.

## Spring: the most photogenic season Vancouver has

**Cherry blossom season** (roughly late March through April) is a gift to any brand that shoots content here. The Vancouver Cherry Blossom Festival gives it structure, but the real opportunity is visual: Vancouver has more than 40,000 cherry trees, according to [Destination Vancouver](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/inspirations/city/20-best-places-to-see-cherry-blossoms-around-vancouver), and the whole city turns into a set. Book your content shoots for these weeks — the backdrop does half the work.

**Vaisakhi** (Wednesday, April 14, 2027) brings some of the largest parades in North America to Vancouver and Surrey, and it's a significant community moment for the region's large Punjabi Sikh population. Organizers reported more than 450,000 attendees at the 2026 Surrey parade ([release](https://www.surreyvaisakhiparade.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Release-2026-Khalsa-Day-Parade-Results.pdf)), where families and businesses hand out free food along the route as seva, or selfless service. Our reading: show up through service and [community sponsorship](/blog/local-partnerships-sponsorship-marketing/), not a sales pitch. **Mother's Day** in May closes the season as one of the year's strongest retail and dining occasions.

## Summer: the festival gauntlet

Summer compresses a lot into ten weeks: **Canada Day**, **BC Day**, the outdoor festival circuit, fireworks at English Bay, and the **PNE Fair**, which in 2026 ran from August 22 to Labour Day, September 7 ([PNE](https://www.pne.ca/files/uploads/2026/04/Release-2026-Fair-Programming.pdf)).

One change: the three-night Honda Celebration of Light [was cancelled for 2026](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/celebration-of-light-vancouver-fireworks-2026-cancelled) over funding, replaced by a one-night show, [Summer Lights in English Bay](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/inspirations/family-and-kids/fireworks-return-to-vancouver-with-summer-lights-in-english-bay), on July 31. No 2027 fireworks dates had been announced when we checked in November 2026.

The trap here is volume — summer offers so many moments that brands try to touch all of them and end up memorable at none. Pick the one or two that match where your audience actually is, and go deep. If you're hosting your own event around one of them, our [three-phase event framework](/blog/event-marketing-guide-three-phase-framework/) covers the before, during and after.

## Fall: the commercial engine

Fall is where the calendar gets dense and the stakes get commercial: **back-to-school** in September, **Mid-Autumn Festival** and its mooncake and gifting season (September 15 in 2027), **Thanksgiving** in October, **Halloween**, **Diwali** (typically October or November — a major moment across Metro Vancouver's South Asian communities; in 2027 it lands in the same week as Halloween), then the **Black Friday/Cyber Monday** corridor straight into **Christmas market season**. These moments are competitive precisely because they convert. The brands that win them locked their creative in September, not mid-November.

One date in this stretch is not a sales moment: the **National Day for Truth and Reconciliation** (September 30), a BC statutory holiday since 2023, which the [Province of BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/indigenous-people/national-day-for-truth-and-reconciliation) describes as a day to commemorate and learn about the legacy of residential schools. Plan for the closure; leave out the promotions.

## Key 2027 dates at a glance

Statutory holidays ("stat") are from the [Province of BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/statutory-holidays); lunar dates are from the [Hong Kong Observatory](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/calendar/text/files/T2027e.txt). Ramadan, Eid and Diwali can shift by a day with moon sighting or local almanacs, so confirm with local mosques and temples. "Start by" applies the 6–8 week rule below.

- **New Year's Day** (stat): Friday, January 1, 2027
- **Lunar New Year**: Saturday, February 6, 2027 (start by mid-December 2026)
- **Ramadan**: expected to begin around Monday, February 8, 2027, based on astronomical projections reported by [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/09/09/ramadan-2027-dates-eid-al-fitr-holiday/)
- **Valentine's Day**: Sunday, February 14, 2027
- **Family Day** (stat): Monday, February 15, 2027
- **Lantern Festival**: Saturday, February 20, 2027
- **Eid al-Fitr**: expected Tuesday, March 9 or Wednesday, March 10, 2027
- **Good Friday** (stat): Friday, March 26, 2027; **Easter Sunday**: March 28 (Easter Monday is not a BC statutory holiday)
- **Vaisakhi**: Wednesday, April 14, 2027, per [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/vaisakhi/vaisakhi-date-time.html?year=2027) (start by late February); parade dates are announced by the organizers
- **Mother's Day**: Sunday, May 9, 2027 (start by mid-March)
- **Victoria Day** (stat): Monday, May 24, 2027
- **Canada Day** (stat): Thursday, July 1, 2027
- **BC Day** (stat): Monday, August 2, 2027
- **English Bay fireworks and PNE Fair**: 2027 dates not announced as of November 2026
- **Labour Day** (stat): Monday, September 6, 2027
- **Mid-Autumn Festival**: Wednesday, September 15, 2027 (start by late July)
- **National Day for Truth and Reconciliation** (stat): Thursday, September 30, 2027
- **Thanksgiving** (stat): Monday, October 11, 2027
- **Diwali**: Friday, October 29, 2027 by the New Delhi almanac ([Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/lakshmipuja/festivals-lakshmipuja-timings.html)); the observed date can differ by a day outside India, so check with local temples (start by early September)
- **Halloween**: Sunday, October 31, 2027
- **Remembrance Day** (stat): Thursday, November 11, 2027
- **Black Friday / Cyber Monday**: November 26 and 29, 2027
- **Christmas Day** (stat): Saturday, December 25, 2027 (Boxing Day is not a BC statutory holiday)

## The multicultural layer isn't optional

Notice what a Metro Vancouver calendar looks like when you build it honestly: Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, Diwali, Vaisakhi, and Ramadan and Eid sit alongside the statutory holidays as first-class moments. That's not a diversity gesture — it's demographic reality. A calendar that only contains the holidays printed on a bank calendar is marketing to a city that doesn't exist.

## Three rules that make the calendar work

**Plan 6-8 weeks back from every moment.** Creative, partnerships, and organic build-up all need runway. A moment you start working on the week it happens is a moment you've missed.

**Choose 6-8 moments, not all of them.** Filter every occasion through one question: does our brand have something genuine to say here? Six moments done with real creative beat twenty template posts. Owning a moment also means coming back to it the same way every year: Tim Hortons' [Roll Up the Rim](/blog/tim-hortons-roll-up-the-rim-teardown/) became a ritual partly because it shows up for a limited window and then leaves.

**Keep the evergreen base running.** Seasonal spikes should sit on top of consistent weekly content, not replace it. Roughly two-thirds evergreen, one-third seasonal keeps you visible between peaks — our guide to [building a content calendar](/blog/content-calendar-for-small-business/) covers the system side.
